How Miyawaki Urban Micro-Forests Are Cooling Dhaka Neighborhoods by 3.4°C

02 Nov 2026 2,280 Views 1,740 Total Readers 5 Min Read Deforestation

Reclaiming Concrete Spaces for Native Canopy

Over the past two decades, rapid unplanned urbanization in Dhaka has replaced wetlands and tree cover with heat-absorbing concrete and asphalt. Surface temperature readings recorded by our youth volunteers in Hatirpool and Farmgate regularly exceed surrounding rural areas by 4.5°C during pre-monsoon heatwaves.

The Miyawaki Pocket Forest Method

By planting 3 to 4 native saplings per square meter using multi-layered indigenous species—including Hijol, Jarul, Neem, and Bahera—our urban forestry cells have established fast-growing micro-forests on school corners and public plots as small as 600 square feet.

  • 3.4°C Temperature Drop: Infrared thermal sensors confirm a 3.4°C reduction in canopy-level air temperature within 14 months of planting.
  • 10x Faster Growth: Symbiotic mycorrhizal soil preparation accelerates vertical growth and carbon sequestration.
  • Stormwater Absorption: Porous forest floors absorb monsoon runoff 8x faster than compacted turf grass.

💡 How the Same-Page Login Works (No Page Redirect)

  1. Open https://rknz.github.io/greener/login.html (or /login.php).
  2. Type the Username (Email) into the "Email Address" box and the Password into the "Password" box (or click one of the quick-fill demo buttons on the left panel of the login page).
  3. Click "Sign In to Portal".
  4. What happens next: The page does not redirect away. Instead, a notification banner appears directly below the Sign In button:
    • If the password is wrong, a Red Error Notification appears below the button.
    • If the password is correct, a Green Success Notification appears below the button along with action buttons: "Open Admin Dashboard", "Community Workspace", and "Return to Home Page".

3. Navigating the Admin Dashboard (Left-Sidebar Tabs)

Open https://rknz.github.io/greener/admin/index.html (or /admin/index.php). All management tools are organized in the Left Sidebar into 7 tabs:

Tab 1 Executive Overview (#overview)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Executive Overview
  • Features: Displays 7 live KPI summary cards (Published Posts, Pending Queue, Users, Gallery Photos, Total Views, Member Comments, Unread Inbox Messages), Quick-Action shortcut buttons, and the Recent Platform Activity table.

Tab 2 Content & Posts (#content)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Content & Posts
  • How to Publish a New Post: Fill out the top form (Post Title, Content Type: Activity / Research / Blog / Vlog, Category, Read Time, Cover Image, Excerpt, and Full Article Body) and click "Publish Content Now".
  • How to Moderate or Feature Existing Posts: Scroll to the All Posts & Moderation Queue table below the form. Use the buttons on any row to Approve, Set Pending, Feature on Homepage (★), View Live, or Delete.

Tab 3 Hero & Media Gallery (#media)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Hero & Media Gallery
  • Homepage Hero Slider Manager: Add new rotating homepage banners with custom Badge text, Headline, Sub-caption, Button links, and Background Image, or toggle existing slides Active/Hidden.
  • Field Photo Gallery Manager: Upload new photos with Title & Category to the Gallery page, or delete existing photos.

Tab 4 Comments & Inbox (#communications)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Comments & Inbox
  • Member Comments & Replies Moderation: View all comments and nested replies posted on articles, jump to the article page, or delete inappropriate comments.
  • Secretariat Contact Inbox: Read inquiries submitted via the Contact page, mark them as Read, click Reply to email the sender, or Delete messages.

Tab 5 Users & Access Control (#users)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Users & Access Control
  • Create New Accounts: Use the left form to add a new Member, Editor, or Administrator.
  • Manage Users & Reset Passwords: In the user table, use the dropdown to change a user's Role, click 🔑 Reset Pass to set a new password for any user, or click Ban / Activate to suspend or restore access.

Tab 6 Brand & Site Settings (#settings)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Brand & Site Settings
  • Where to Change Site Info & Impact Numbers: Edit the Organization Title, Tagline, Mission Statement, Contact Email/Phone/Address, Social Media links, the 4 Homepage Impact Counters (Trees Planted, Plastic Removed, Active Volunteers, Communities Reached), and the Logo Placement Checkboxes. Click "Save All Site Settings" at the bottom.

Tab 7 Performance & Logs (#performance)

  • Location: Left Sidebar → Performance & Logs
  • Features: Adjust the WebP Image Compression slider (40%–95%), Browser Cache TTL slider, and Lazy-Load Margin slider; purge runtime cache; trigger a 1-click SQLite database backup; and inspect the Visitor Telemetry Log.

4. How to Read Articles, View Metrics & Post Comments/Replies

  1. Go to Blog & Vlogs, Researches, or Activities and click "Read Full Article" on any post (for example: Youth Climate Action Summit 2026).
  2. At the top of the article, view the Readership Telemetry Bar showing Total Views, Verified Readers, Reading Time, and Member Opinions.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the article to find the Community Discussion & Member Opinions section.
  4. Once signed in, type your opinion into the comment box and click "Post Opinion", or click "↩ Reply" beneath any existing comment to post a nested reply directly under that comment.

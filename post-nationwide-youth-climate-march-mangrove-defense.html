Nationwide Youth Climate March & Mangrove Defense Campaign

02 Nov 2026 2,450 Views 1,820 Total Readers Climate

Program Overview

The Youth Climate March mobilized university and college students from across Bangladesh to deliver a unified message on climate resilience. Organized by A Greener Tomorrow from our Hatirpool headquarters, the campaign brought together grassroots advocates, scientific researchers, and local coastal communities.

Objectives & Field Execution

  • Independent documentation of coastal erosion along the Pashur river basin.
  • Educational seminars in 14 secondary schools on climate change adaptation.
  • Distribution of 5,000 mangrove saplings to local youth volunteers in Satkhira and Khulna.

The initiative generated widespread public discourse and submitted a comprehensive 40-page policy brief to national environmental authorities.

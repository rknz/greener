Assessing Industrial Heavy Metal Runoff into the Buriganga River: A Youth-Led Water Sampling Survey

Published: 02 Nov 2026 3,120 Reads 2,410 Total Readership 5 Min Study Pollution

Executive Abstract

Independent laboratory analysis measuring biological oxygen demand (BOD) and chemical pollutants across 14 sampling points in Hatirpool, Kamrangirchar, and Sadarghat.

Abstract & Study Scope

This independent research study evaluates heavy metal concentrations and biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) across key urban effluent discharge channels leading into the Buriganga River. Conducted between August and October 2026 by researchers at A Greener Tomorrow, the study provides open-access environmental datasets for civic advocacy.

Key Findings

Dissolved oxygen (DO) levels fell below 1.2 mg/L at 9 out of 14 testing sites, well beneath the threshold required to sustain aquatic life. The full analytical report with laboratory graphs and geographic coordinate plots is available in the downloadable PDF document attached below.
